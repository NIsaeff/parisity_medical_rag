Search results without and with reranking

Real results from the King James Bible, chunked one chapter per vector. On the left is plain cosine search, where the query and each chapter were embedded separately. On the right, the same candidates are rescored by a reranker that reads the query and each chapter together. Only the rows above the dashed line would reach the LLM.

Without rerank: cosine similarity

With rerank:

The same candidates, re-read together with the query

Why fetch 25 to keep 5? A reranker can only reorder what retrieval already found. Fetch too few and the right chapter never gets a vote. Why do some rows say ✂? The reranker reads about 1,024 tokens of query plus chapter, so a long chapter is judged on its opening only. That's the cost of big chunks.
